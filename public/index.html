How it works

From upload to a 30-day report

  1. 01

    Describe the song

    Genre, mood, tempo, who it sounds like and where you're from. DropRadar reads the sound and tells you where it is most likely to travel.

  2. 02

    Get matched

    Every outlet is scored against your song. You see why each one fits and get a personal pitch written for it, ready for your approval.

  3. 03

    Follow the rollout

    A day-by-day plan from four weeks before release to a month after. Each step explains why it matters, so you learn the game as you play it.

  4. 04

    See what worked

    Your smart link tracks which posts, platforms and outlets brought real listeners and fans you own by email.

Release protection

Security built in, because releases attract scammers

Fake promoters sell “guaranteed streams” that get songs removed and royalties withheld. Account takeovers spike around release day. DropRadar watches for both.

  • Promoter scanner

    Paste any promo DM or email. It flags guaranteed-stream offers, pay-per-play pricing, login requests and pressure tactics.

  • Spike alerts

    Sudden traffic jumps that look like bot activity are flagged so you can report them before the platforms penalise you.

  • Account checklist

    Two-factor login, separate business email, team access without shared passwords.

What DropRadar will never do

Sell you a number of streams, buy playlist placements, or use bots. Every listener it helps you reach is a real person who chose to press play.
